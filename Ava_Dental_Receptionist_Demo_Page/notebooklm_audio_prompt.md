# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

Use the source files from the Ava Dental Receptionist Starter Kit and create a friendly, professional audio overview for dental practice owners.

Audience:

- Dental office owners
- Practice managers
- Front desk leads

Tone:

- Clear
- Warm
- Practical
- Lightly fun
- Not technical

Main message:

> Ava is an AI receptionist demo that helps dental offices answer missed or after-hours calls, collect new patient details, route urgent dental concerns, and book appointments.

Explain:

1. The problem: dental offices miss calls when the front desk is busy or closed.
2. Why missed calls matter: a missed call can become a missed new patient.
3. What Ava does: answers, qualifies, collects, books, and summarizes.
4. What Ava does not do: diagnose, prescribe, give clinical advice, or replace the office team.
5. How the demo works: call Ava, ask to schedule a cleaning, then ask about tooth pain.
6. The pilot offer: start small with after-hours or missed-call backup.

Include this simple analogy:

> Think of Ava like a front-desk safety net. She catches calls that would otherwise fall through the cracks.

End with:

> The best first step is a short pilot: start with after-hours calls, tune Ava to the office, and see whether she captures appointment opportunities the practice would have missed.
