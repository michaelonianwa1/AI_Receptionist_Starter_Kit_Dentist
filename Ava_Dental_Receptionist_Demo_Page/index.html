FAQ

Simple answers before the live demo

Does this replace my team?

No. It works as a backup layer for missed calls, after-hours calls, and routine first-step intake.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, when connected to the right calendar and booking rules. We demonstrate this during the live walkthrough.

Can it answer business questions?

Yes. It can answer approved FAQs such as hours, location, services, and basic next steps.

What about sensitive requests?

The system can be configured with guardrails so sensitive or urgent situations are routed to the right human process.
