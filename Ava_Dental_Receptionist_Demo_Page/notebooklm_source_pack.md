## The Problem

Businesses miss calls when:

- the front desk is busy
- staff are at lunch
- the office is closed
- several callers come in at once
- follow-up is slow

For appointment-based businesses, a missed call can become missed revenue.

## What The AI Receptionist Does

The AI receptionist can:

- greet callers professionally
- ask simple intake questions
- collect name, phone number, email, and reason for calling
- answer approved FAQs
- identify the right appointment type or follow-up path
- book appointments when connected to a calendar
- summarize calls for the team
- route urgent requests based on business rules

## What It Does Not Do

For healthcare-style businesses, the AI receptionist should not:

- diagnose
- prescribe
- give clinical advice
- make treatment decisions
- promise insurance coverage
- quote exact medical or dental pricing unless approved by the business

Instead, it collects information, follows approved scripts, and routes the caller to the right human process.

The system can be configured with:

## FAQ

Q: Does this replace my team?

A: No. It works as a backup layer for missed calls, after-hours calls, and routine first-step intake.

Q: Can it book appointments?

A: Yes, when connected to the right calendar and booking rules.

Q: Can it answer business questions?

A: Yes. It can answer approved FAQs such as hours, location, services, and next steps.

Q: What about sensitive requests?

A: The system can be configured with guardrails so sensitive or urgent situations are routed to the right human process.
