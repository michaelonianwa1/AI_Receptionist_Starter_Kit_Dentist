# NotebookLM Video Overview Prompt

Create a short video script and scene outline for a 60-90 second product explainer.

Product:

> Ava Dental AI Receptionist

Audience:

- Dental practice owners
- Office managers

Style:

- Professional
- Simple
- Friendly
- Demonstration-focused
- No hype

Video structure:

1. Opening scene: busy dental front desk, phone ringing.
2. Text/title: "Missed calls can become missed patients."
3. Show Ava answering an after-hours call.
4. Show patient asking: "I am a new patient and want to schedule a cleaning."
5. Show Ava collecting name, phone, email, and appointment reason.
6. Show calendar booking: New Patient Exam and Cleaning.
7. Show urgent routing example: tooth pain and swelling.
8. Show office notification/call summary.
9. Close with pilot offer: "Start with after-hours or missed-call backup."

Narration should include:

> Ava helps your office answer calls, collect patient information, route urgent concerns, and book appointments while your team stays in control.

Safety line:

> Ava does not diagnose or give medical advice. She follows your approved office rules and routes urgent concerns appropriately.

Call to action:

> Ask for a 5-minute demo.
