# Ava Demo Walkthrough

Use this when you are speaking with a dental office owner or office manager.

## Goal

Keep the demo short, fun, and practical. You are not trying to explain all of AI. You are showing how the office can stop losing easy appointment opportunities.

## 5-Minute Demo Flow

### 1. Open Warmly

Say:

> Thanks for taking a few minutes. I built a simple AI receptionist demo for dental offices. The idea is not to replace your front desk. It is to catch the calls your team misses when they are busy, at lunch, or closed.

### 2. Name The Pain

Say:

> Every missed call could be a new patient. Ava helps answer, collect details, route the call, and book the right appointment.

### 3. Make It Fun

Say:

> Let us pretend I am a patient calling after hours. I am going to ask Ava for a cleaning, then I will ask her about tooth pain so you can see how she handles something urgent.

### 4. Run Test Call 1

Say to Ava:

> I am a new patient and want to schedule a cleaning.

Watch for Ava to:

- greet you
- ask whether you are new or existing
- collect name, phone, email
- offer appointment times
- route to New Patient Exam and Cleaning

### 5. Run Test Call 2

Say to Ava:

> I have bad tooth pain and my cheek is swollen.

Watch for Ava to:

- show empathy
- avoid diagnosis
- ask urgency questions
- route to urgent dental follow-up
- recommend 911/ER for dangerous symptoms

### 6. Show The Business Result

Say:

> This is the part that matters. Your team gets the appointment request and call summary. The patient feels answered instead of abandoned.

### 7. Ask Simple Questions

Ask:

1. How many calls do you think your office misses in a normal week?
2. Do you get after-hours appointment requests?
3. Would you rather test this after hours, during lunch, or only when calls are missed?

### 8. Offer The Pilot

Say:

> I am offering this as a small 60-day pilot for a few practices. We start with after-hours or missed-call backup only, tune it with your office FAQs, and measure whether it captures real appointment opportunities.

Simple pilot:

- $300 setup
- $300/month
- after-hours or missed-call backup
- 30 days of tuning

## Professional Safety Language

Say:

> Ava does not diagnose, prescribe, or give medical advice. She schedules, collects basic information, answers approved FAQs, and routes urgent concerns to your team or emergency guidance.
