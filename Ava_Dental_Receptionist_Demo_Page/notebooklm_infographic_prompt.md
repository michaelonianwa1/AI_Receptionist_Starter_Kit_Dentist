# NotebookLM Infographic Prompt

Create a one-page infographic for dental offices.

Title:

> Ava Dental AI Receptionist

Subtitle:

> A simple after-hours and missed-call backup for dental practices.

Sections:

## 1. The Problem

- Front desk teams get busy.
- Patients call after hours.
- Missed calls can mean missed appointments.

## 2. What Ava Does

- Answers common calls
- Collects name, phone, email, and reason for visit
- Routes appointment type
- Books into the calendar
- Sends call summary to the office

## 3. Demo Appointment Types

- New Patient Exam and Cleaning
- Emergency Dental Consult
- Invisalign Consultation
- Teeth Whitening Consultation

## 4. Safety Guardrails

- No diagnosis
- No medical advice
- No pricing promises
- Urgent symptoms routed appropriately

## 5. Pilot Offer

- Start after hours or missed calls only
- 60-day founding client pilot
- Setup + monthly management

Visual direction:

- Clean healthcare look
- Use teal, blue, white, and a small yellow accent
- Use simple icons: phone, calendar, shield, clipboard, smile
- Make it easy to understand in 10 seconds
