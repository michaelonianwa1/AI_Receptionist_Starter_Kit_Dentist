# Dentist Demo Script

Use this when showing the AI receptionist to a dentist or office manager.

## Opening

> Most dental offices lose money from missed calls, after-hours calls, and slow follow-up. This AI receptionist helps answer those calls, collect patient details, book appointments, and alert your team.

## Set Expectation

> This is not replacing your front desk. It handles the repetitive first step so your team can focus on patients, treatment plans, and high-value conversations.

## Live Demo Call 1: New Patient

Say to the AI:

> Hi, I am a new patient and I want to schedule a cleaning.

Let the AI:

- ask if you are a new patient
- collect name, phone, email
- ask for preferred time
- offer appointment slots
- confirm booking

Then show:

- contact record
- appointment
- internal notification
- SMS confirmation

## Live Demo Call 2: Emergency

Say:

> I have really bad tooth pain and my cheek is swollen. Can someone see me today?

Expected AI behavior:

- show empathy
- ask brief safety questions
- avoid diagnosing
- offer urgent appointment or transfer
- notify the office urgently

## Live Demo Call 3: Insurance and Price

Say:

> Do you take Delta Dental and how much is a cleaning?

Expected AI behavior:

- explain that the office accepts many PPO plans if configured
- say the team can verify benefits
- avoid promising exact cost
- guide caller to book an exam

## Close

> The value is simple: if this books even one or two extra patients per month, it can pay for itself. We can start with after-hours calls or missed calls only, so your team stays in control.

## Discovery Questions To Ask The Dentist

1. How many calls do you miss per week?
2. Do you get many after-hours appointment requests?
3. How fast does your team usually follow up?
4. Which appointment type is most valuable for new patients?
5. Do you want the AI to book directly or only collect details first?
6. Who should receive urgent notifications?
7. What would one extra new patient per week be worth?
