# Sales Offer and Pricing

## Simple Offer

> I set up an AI receptionist for dental offices that answers calls, captures new patient details, books appointments, and alerts your team, especially when your front desk is busy or closed.

## Starter Package

Price:

- Setup: $500-$1,500
- Monthly management: $300-$750/month
- Usage: client pays call/SMS/email usage, or you include a small allowance

Includes:

- AI receptionist setup
- one phone number or forwarding setup
- new patient booking flow
- urgent call routing
- CRM contact capture
- appointment notifications
- 30 days of tuning

## First Client Founding Offer

Use this if you need your first case study:

- Setup: $300
- Monthly: $300/month
- Term: 60 days
- Includes: after-hours AI receptionist and appointment request capture

Position it as:

> I am taking on three local dental offices as founding clients at a lower rate in exchange for feedback and a case study if it works well.

## Stronger Package

Price:

- Setup: $1,500-$3,000
- Monthly: $750-$1,500/month

Includes:

- inbound AI receptionist
- missed-call text back
- new patient booking
- emergency routing
- review request automation
- reactivation campaign for old leads/patients
- monthly reporting

## ROI Talking Point

If a new dental patient is worth $500-$2,000+ over time, the AI only needs to help save a few missed opportunities per month to be profitable.

Avoid promising exact revenue. Say:

> The goal is to capture opportunities that are already calling you.
