# Dentist AI Receptionist Starter Kit

This kit helps you build and demo an AI receptionist for a dental office using GrowthHub365 or a similar HighLevel-style platform.

## What You Are Selling

You are not selling "AI." You are selling fewer missed calls, faster booking, and cleaner follow-up.

Simple offer:

> We set up an AI receptionist for your dental office that answers missed or after-hours calls, answers basic questions, captures new patient details, books appointments, and alerts your team.

## Best First Use Case

Start with inbound calls for new patient appointment requests.

The receptionist should:

- greet callers professionally
- identify whether they are a new or existing patient
- answer basic office questions
- collect name, phone, email, reason for visit, insurance status, and preferred appointment time
- book a new patient consultation or hygiene appointment
- transfer urgent dental emergencies to the office or emergency line
- send a follow-up text or email
- create/update the contact in the CRM
