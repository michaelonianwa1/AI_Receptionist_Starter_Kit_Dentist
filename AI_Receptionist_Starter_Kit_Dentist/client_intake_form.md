## Emergency Rules

What should happen if the caller says they have:

- severe tooth pain
- facial swelling
- uncontrolled bleeding
- dental trauma
- knocked-out tooth
- fever or infection symptoms
- trouble breathing or swallowing

Recommended rule:

> If the caller has trouble breathing, trouble swallowing, severe swelling, or uncontrolled bleeding, tell them to call 911 or go to the emergency room. For other urgent dental concerns, collect details and transfer to the office or emergency line.
